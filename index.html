Why now

Three pillars

Israel stands on three pillars.

  • Security

    Defence against enemies larger than us.

  • Economy

    Growth, jobs and state revenue.

  • Alliances

    Diplomatic, security and economic backing.

But what do all three stand on?

One foundation

All three stand on one foundation: a technological edge.

  • Security

    A qualitative edge over larger enemies, built on people, technology and intelligence.

  • Economy

    High-tech was about 58% of Israel's exports in 2025 and about half of its economic growth.

  • Alliances

    In December 2025, Israel was one of the first countries to sign Pax Silica, the US-led initiative for the AI supply chain.

Erode the foundation, and all three pillars weaken at once.

Sources: Israel Innovation Authority, The State of High-Tech 2026; US Department of State, Pax Silica, December 2025

Erosion

In the age of AI, that foundation is eroding.

  • Code is becoming a commodity

    The edge of engineers who ship fast is shrinking against every competitor.

  • Value is moving to costly layers

    Models, chips and energy: a game of capital and scale.

  • Talent is being pulled abroad

    Frontier labs abroad draw the very best. In 2025 Israel saw a net outflow of AI talent, and R&D jobs in Israel fell for the first time in a decade.

Israel is not weak. It is losing its relative edge.

Sources: Stanford AI Index 2026; Israel Innovation Authority, The State of High-Tech 2026

The price

And it is already costing us.

  • Chips

    In January 2025, a US rule placed Israel, with most countries, under a per-country cap on advanced AI chips: about 50,000 through 2027. It was rescinded that May, before it was enforced.

  • Frontier models

    In June 2026, a US export directive suspended foreign nationals' access to Mythos 5, which Anthropic described as its strongest model for cybersecurity. When access returned, it came back first for a set of US organizations.

  • Competition

    In May 2025, Saudi Arabia and the UAE announced large AI chip deals with US companies and a planned 5-gigawatt AI campus in Abu Dhabi.

Access to the most powerful technology is a political decision. A country seen as just another country waits in line.

Sources: US Federal Register (AI Diffusion Rule, January 2025); US Department of Commerce, May 2025; Anthropic, June and July 2026; company announcements, May 2025

The lever

So our one lever is people.

  • Chips and energy

    We will not win here. The US and the Gulf are larger than us by orders of magnitude.

  • Capital

    We will not win here. Funds and labs at a scale with no match in Israel.

  • Talent

    Here we lead: Israel has the highest concentration of AI talent among the countries measured.

The talent is already here. The question is whether we direct it, or lose it.

Source: Stanford AI Index 2026 (LinkedIn data, 2025)

The missing piece

Universities educate. Elite units train. Companies hire. Investors finance.

No institution owns the journey from exceptional builder to national capability.

The problem is not talent. It's infrastructure.
